What Is Human Trafficking?

Human trafficking is a crime that involves compelling a person to provide labor, services, or commercial sex through force, fraud, or coercion. It can happen to anyone, regardless of age, gender, nationality, or background — in cities, suburbs, and rural areas alike.

Recognize the Warning Signs

Trafficking is often hidden in plain sight. A person may be a victim if they show several of these signs:

Physical Signs

  • Signs of physical abuse or malnourishment
  • Untreated illness or injuries
  • Exhaustion or signs of confinement

Behavioral Signs

  • Fearful, anxious, or submissive around a "boss" or partner
  • Scripted or rehearsed answers to questions
  • Avoids eye contact or speaking for themselves

Situational Signs

  • Does not have control of their own ID or money
  • Lives with employer or many people in a cramped space
  • Not allowed to speak alone or come and go freely
